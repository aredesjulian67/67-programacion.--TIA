Tradition and culture

Traditional Festivals

Discover the most representative celebrations of Villa General Belgrano.

Easter

Strudel Festival

Season: Easter (March / April)

Location: Events and Convention Center

Details: Central European pastries including Sacher, strudel, and traditional sweets.

Winter

Chocolate Festival

Season: Winter holidays (July)

Location: Events and Convention Hall

Details: Free giant hot chocolate for children, chocolate fountains, magic, and children's shows.

October

Oktoberfest

Season: October (the long weekend around October 12)

Location: Beer Park

Details: The Espiche ceremony, the Black Monk, parades, and craft beer tastings.

Tango

Tango and Thematic Nights

Season: Nighttime events throughout the season

Location: The village center and cultural spaces

Details: Tango, live music, dancing, and evenings of art and tradition.

May 14-16, 2027

Valhöll Ultra Trail

Overview: One of Argentina's most demanding and epic trail running races, based in Villa General Belgrano, Córdoba.

Distances: 7K (~9 km | +354 m), 12K (~13.3 km | +642 m), 21K (~23 km | +1,077 m), 30K (~33 km | +1,630 m), 42K (~44.7 km | +2,048 m), and 60K (~59.5 km | +2,718 m).

Mini Valhöll: A recreational category designed especially for children.

Location: Villa General Belgrano and the Calamuchita Valley, with views of Cerro Champaquí and Los Molinos Lake.

Details: Main activities, registration, and parallel events take place at the Events and Convention Hall. The race follows a strict semi-self-sufficiency system, requiring runners to carry safety equipment according to their distance.

September 19-20, 2026

Lavender Festival

Overview: An innovative, sensory, and growing event organized by the municipality together with the local Die Lavendel venture.

Schedule: Saturday from 2:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. and Sunday from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

Location: Events and Convention Hall, Villa General Belgrano. Admission is free.

Exhibitors: More than 30 exhibitors from across the country offering natural cosmetics, handmade soaps, essential oils, soy candles, floral waters, themed ceramics, and wellness products.

Activities: Lavender-inspired food and tastings, live cooking and masterclasses, talks about distillation and agroecological growing, aromatherapy workshops, acoustic music, and more than 30 prize drawings.

Lavender tourism: Visitors can explore nearby lavender fields in the Calamuchita Valley and learn how the plant is harvested and distilled.

History and Customs

German roots

Villa General Belgrano was shaped by Central European immigrants, especially Germans and Austrians, who left their mark on its architecture, cuisine, and celebrations.

Cuisine

Traditional food accompanies every celebration: beer, baked goods, German dishes, desserts such as strudel, and after-dinner conversations inspired by Alpine landscapes.

Music and dance

Parades, folk groups, and live music bring every event to life, turning the village into a place of community, tradition, and shared joy.

Local tourism

During these celebrations, the village welcomes visitors from across the country and abroad, strengthening the region's cultural, commercial, and tourism activity.
