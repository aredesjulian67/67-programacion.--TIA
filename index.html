What else can you do?

Cuisine

Try traditional dishes such as strudel, Sacher, craft beer, and local chocolate at the village's many food venues.

Visit cultural routes, museums, plazas, and historic streets to discover Villa General Belgrano's Central European identity.

Family and activities

The festivals offer something for every age: shows, live music, children's activities, street performances, and tastings.
